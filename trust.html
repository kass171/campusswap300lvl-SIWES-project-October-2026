⚠️ Safety rules

  • Never pay money for a swap — it's always free
  • Meet in public campus spaces only
  • Report no-shows or bad actors — we ban quickly
  • Your number stays private until you accept a swap
  • If it feels wrong, don't do it. Trust your gut.
